# Yardfolk Garden Care — Lane Cove, Sydney

A completely separate gardening homepage concept with its own branding, content, images and layout.

## Open the site

Open `dist/index.html` in a browser. In the downloadable ZIP, `index.html` is at the top level. All images and fonts are local. No installation or build is required.

## Design direction

- Deep plum #2B1024, rich coral #DF5340 and white
- Bold condensed display typography with clean sans-serif body text
- Oversized typographic opening, tilted photographic frame and circular enquiry link
- Interactive garden care menu with four large photographic cards, all visible on the homepage
- Garden-life editorial sections, a wide crew scene, local service areas and FAQs
- A sentence-style enquiry preview

The fictional brand is Yardfolk Garden Care, based around Lane Cove, Sydney. Copy and all eight images were created for this concept. No previous website images or content were reused.

## Package contents

- Complete HTML, CSS and JavaScript website
- Web-optimised WebP images
- `source-images/`: eight original generated PNGs
- `editable-photo-masters/`: three native-resolution, lossless Photoshop PSD photo masters; each has one named editable photo layer and a composite preview
- `IMAGE-PROMPTS.json` and `IMAGE-PROMPTS-ADDITIONAL-FIVE.json`: exact image briefs
- Font licence and locally hosted font files
- `brand-tokens.json`: colour and typography reference

The PSDs are photo masters, not layered mockups of the entire page. They preserve every source pixel and have not been upscaled. They use uncompressed layer and composite data for compatibility. The ZIP stores these PSDs directly; the remaining files are compressed. Original PNGs and editable masters are separate from the lightweight site assets.

## Homepage imagery

Eight distinct photographs are visible on the homepage: hero planting, lawn care, seasonal tidy-up, hedge pruning, pot planting, hands in soil, garden crew and courtyard life. Images are not hidden inside service panels or a carousel.

## Original editorial image dimensions

- Yard scene: 1448 × 1086
- Hands and soil: 1122 × 1402
- Courtyard: 1536 × 1024

## Enquiry form

This is a design preview. The form validates the fields and displays a summary in the page. No information is sent, saved or stored. Connect a real enquiry service before using it to collect leads.

## Before client launch

Confirm the final business name, service scope, service areas and contact details with the client. The images are illustrative rather than records of real staff or completed jobs. No registrations, reviews, prices or years of experience have been claimed.

## Verification

Verified local asset paths, anchor targets, image dimensions and decoding, form labels, care-menu/service mappings, JavaScript syntax and ZIP integrity. PSD composites were decoded with Pillow and compared pixel-for-pixel with the source images. Responsive breakpoints support mobile, tablet and desktop. Live browser rendering was not available in the static-site preview environment.
