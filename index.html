BEFORE WE GET OUR HANDS DIRTY

A FEW
GOOD QUESTIONS.

Is my garden too small?

Courtyards, small beds and compact outdoor spaces are welcome. Tell us what you have and what needs attention so we can discuss the work.

Can you help just once?

Yes. A one-off visit can be a good way to catch up on jobs or get ready for a new season. We’ll agree on the scope before getting started.

How often would my garden need care?

It depends on the planting, lawn, season and how you like to use the space. We can talk through a routine that suits those needs.

What happens to the clippings?

Let us know about your green-bin space or composting arrangements. Waste handling can then be discussed as part of the agreed work.
